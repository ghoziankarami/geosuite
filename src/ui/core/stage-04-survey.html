Survey — Downhole Deviation

Dip and azimuth at given depths per hole. Without a survey the hole is treated as vertical (dip −90, azimuth 0).

Standards and survey method

Downhole surveys are required per JORC 2012 1.16-1.17. Survey intervals should be regular (typically every 30 m for diamond drill, every 10 m for RC) per KCMI Ch1 1.2. Minimum one survey per hole; multiple surveys for inclined or deflected holes. The survey method (single-shot, multi-shot, gyro) must be reported.
