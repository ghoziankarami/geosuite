Section — Multi-Hole Correlation

Section view: pick azimuth + origin + corridor half-width, and the system projects holes inside the corridor onto the section line. Each hole is drawn as a vertical strip (or tilted by dip) with lithology fill.

Limitation: Section uses orthogonal projection to the section plane, not true 3D intersection. For holes with dip < 60° (highly inclined), positions may be inaccurate by up to 10% of deviation. For final interpretation, use commercial 3D modeling software.
